## 4. EMPIRICAL REGULATORY STATISTICS
- **₹250 Cr DPDP Penalty:** The Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 prescribes statutory penalties up to ₹250 Crore for security lapses.
  *Source Attribution:* `DPDP Act 2023 Gazette of India | meity.gov.in`

- **6-Hour CERT-In Rule:** CERT-In Cyber Security Directions mandate reporting of cybersecurity incidents strictly within 6 hours.
  *Source Attribution:* `CERT-In Cyber Incident Reporting Guidelines | cert-in.org.in`

- **1.39M Cyber Incidents:** CERT-In tracked over 1.39 million cybersecurity events across Indian enterprise infrastructures in 2023.
  *Source Attribution:* `Ministry of Electronics & IT Annual Report | meity.gov.in`

- **Third-Party API Risks:** 54% of enterprise cloud breaches stem from unmonitored vendor integrations and unsecured API endpoints.
  *Source Attribution:* `Cloud Security Alliance Enterprise Report | cloudsecurityalliance.org`

- **ISO 27001:2022 Mandate:** Global enterprise procurement teams now disqualify vendors lacking certified ISO 27001 & SOC 2 Type II reports.
  *Source Attribution:* `ISO Global Security Standards Review | iso.org`

- **Zero DPDP Non-Compliance:** End-to-end data mapping, consent architecture, and 6-hour incident workflows establish verifiable cyber hygiene.
  *Source Attribution:* `GOVENICS Cyber & Privacy Practice 2024 | govnics-murex.vercel.app`
