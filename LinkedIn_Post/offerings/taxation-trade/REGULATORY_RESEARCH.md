## 4. EMPIRICAL REGULATORY STATISTICS
- **1.14 Lakh GST Notices:** CBIC issued 1.14 lakh demand notices worth ₹1.45 lakh crore in FY23, largely from GSTR-2B mismatches.
  *Source Attribution:* `CBIC Indirect Tax Enforcement Gazette FY 2022-23 | cbic.gov.in`

- **5x Duty Penalty:** Section 114 of Customs Act 1962 empowers authorities to levy up to five times duty for declaration gaps.
  *Source Attribution:* `Customs Act Enforcement Digest 2023 | cbic.gov.in`

- **₹42,000 Cr Blocked:** Customs valuation disputes under Special Valuation Branch (SVB) lock over ₹42,000 Cr in working capital.
  *Source Attribution:* `CBIC Trade Facilitation Bulletin 2023 | cbic.gov.in`

- **Rule 86B Restriction:** Failure to reconcile GSTR-1 against e-way bills triggers automatic lock on electronic credit ledgers.
  *Source Attribution:* `GST Council Circular No. 170/02/2022-GST | cbic.gov.in`

- **Section 148 Scrutiny:** Faceless tax assessment algorithms cross-examine foreign remittance Form 15CA/CB against customs manifests.
  *Source Attribution:* `CBDT National Faceless Assessment Digest | incometax.gov.in`

- **99.9% ITC Reconciliation:** Automated pre-filing GSTR-2B matching unlocks working capital and defends legitimate input tax credits.
  *Source Attribution:* `GOVENICS Tax & Trade Advisory Benchmarks 2024 | govnics-murex.vercel.app`
