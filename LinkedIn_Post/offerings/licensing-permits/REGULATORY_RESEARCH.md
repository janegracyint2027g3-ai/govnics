## 4. EMPIRICAL REGULATORY STATISTICS
- **38 Statutory Approvals:** A mid-sized manufacturing facility must maintain up to 38 concurrent central, state, and civic licenses.
  *Source Attribution:* `DPIIT Industrial Regulatory Burden Assessment | dpiit.gov.in`

- **₹10 Lakh Fire Penalty:** Operating industrial units without valid Fire NOC risks immediate factory sealing and severe criminal liability.
  *Source Attribution:* `National Building Code & State Fire Services Acts | mha.gov.in`

- **60-Day Renewal Lag:** Manual spreadsheet tracking of operating licenses results in a 60-day average lapse before discovery.
  *Source Attribution:* `Confederation of Indian Industry (CII) Report | cii.in`

- **PESO Explosives Norms:** Petroleum & Explosives Safety Organisation requires strict periodic inspection logs for solvent storage.
  *Source Attribution:* `PESO Annual Operational Circular | peso.gov.in`

- **Boiler Inspection Seals:** Operating industrial boilers past annual certification date triggers immediate factory shutdown orders.
  *Source Attribution:* `Indian Boilers Act 1923 Enforcement Compendium | dpiit.gov.in`

- **100% License Currency:** Automated 60-day advance renewal triggers protect multi-site industrial plants from unexpected halts.
  *Source Attribution:* `GOVENICS Licensing Governance SLA 2024 | govnics-murex.vercel.app`
