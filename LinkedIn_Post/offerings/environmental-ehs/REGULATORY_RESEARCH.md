## 4. EMPIRICAL REGULATORY STATISTICS
- **18.2% Closure Surge:** CPCB recorded an 18.2% annual increase in plant closure directives under Air and Water Acts for industrial units.
  *Source Attribution:* `CPCB Annual Pollution Report FY 2022-23 | cpcb.nic.in`

- **7.46M MT Waste Burden:** Annual industrial hazardous waste mandates barcoded manifest tracking under CPCB HWM Rules.
  *Source Attribution:* `CPCB Hazardous Waste Management Gazette | cpcb.nic.in`

- **₹3 Cr/Day Stoppage:** State Pollution Control Board shutdowns for lapsed Consent to Operate (CTO) cost plants ₹1.5–3 Cr daily.
  *Source Attribution:* `CII Manufacturing & Environmental Survey 2023 | cii.in`

- **CGWA Water Audits:** Central Ground Water Authority mandates digital flow telemetry and recurring bi-annual water impact audits.
  *Source Attribution:* `CGWA Industrial Water Guidelines 2023 | cgwa.gov.in`

- **Section 15 EPA Penalties:** Jan Vishwas Act 2023 replaces jail terms with compounding environmental fiscal penalties up to ₹15 Lakh.
  *Source Attribution:* `Ministry of Environment Gazette Notification | moef.gov.in`

- **100% Consent Currency:** Proactive digital consent renewal workflows eliminate unauthorized discharge penalties before inspections.
  *Source Attribution:* `GOVENICS EHS Compliance Standard 2024 | govnics-murex.vercel.app`
