## 4. EMPIRICAL REGULATORY STATISTICS
- **Top 1,000 Listed BRSR:** SEBI mandates comprehensive Business Responsibility & Sustainability Reporting for top 1,000 entities.
  *Source Attribution:* `SEBI Master Circular on BRSR Disclosures | sebi.gov.in`

- **BRSR Core Value Chain:** From FY 2024-25, top 250 listed companies must provide reasonable ESG assurance across key supply chain partners.
  *Source Attribution:* `SEBI Circular on Assurance of BRSR Core | sebi.gov.in`

- **CBAM Export Tariff:** EU Carbon Border Adjustment Mechanism (CBAM) imposes reporting rules and upcoming carbon tariffs on steel & aluminium.
  *Source Attribution:* `European Commission CBAM Regulations & DGFT Advisory | dgft.gov.in`

- **EPR Plastic Targets:** MoEFCC Plastic Waste Rules mandate 100% recycling certificates and barcoded EPR portal compliance.
  *Source Attribution:* `CPCB EPR Portal Guidance for PIBOs | cpcb.nic.in`

- **Greenwashing Scrutiny:** Regulatory authorities across global markets are actively auditing unsubstantiated corporate net-zero marketing claims.
  *Source Attribution:* `International Organization of Securities Commissions (IOSCO) | iosco.org`

- **Audit-Grade ESG Reporting:** Automated carbon accounting and verified supply-chain data provide institutional investor defensibility.
  *Source Attribution:* `GOVENICS ESG & Sustainability Advisory 2024 | govnics-murex.vercel.app`
