## 4. EMPIRICAL REGULATORY STATISTICS
- **₹50 Lakh CCPA Penalties:** Central Consumer Protection Authority (CCPA) penalizes e-commerce brands up to ₹50 Lakh for deceptive dark patterns.
  *Source Attribution:* `Ministry of Consumer Affairs CCPA Guidelines 2023 | consumeraffairs.nic.in`

- **Legal Metrology Declarations:** Packaged commodities missing statutory MRP, manufacturer address, or consumer care details face warehouse seizures.
  *Source Attribution:* `Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules | consumeraffairs.nic.in`

- **Store EHS & Shop Act:** Retail chains operating across multiple states risk store closures for unrenewed local Shops and Establishments licenses.
  *Source Attribution:* `State Labour Department Retail Directives | labour.gov.in`

- **FMCG Plastic EPR Mandate:** Consumer goods brands must purchase audited EPR certificates to match 100% of packaging plastic placed on market.
  *Source Attribution:* `CPCB Extended Producer Responsibility Register | cpcb.nic.in`

- **Customer Data Privacy:** Retail loyalty programmes and mobile apps must establish verified consent frameworks under DPDP Act 2023.
  *Source Attribution:* `Digital Personal Data Protection Guidelines | meity.gov.in`

- **Multi-Store Compliance SLA:** Centralized multi-state store compliance dashboards and automated packaging verification eliminate retail penalties.
  *Source Attribution:* `GOVENICS Consumer & Retail Practice 2024 | govnics-murex.vercel.app`
