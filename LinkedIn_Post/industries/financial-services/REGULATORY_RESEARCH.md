## 4. EMPIRICAL REGULATORY STATISTICS
- **₹100 Cr+ RBI Penalties:** Reserve Bank of India imposed over ₹100 Crore in cumulative fines on banks & NBFCs for KYC and IT governance lapses in FY23.
  *Source Attribution:* `RBI Enforcement Actions Annual Review FY 2022-23 | rbi.org.in`

- **Scale-Based NBFC Norms:** Upper and Middle Layer NBFCs must implement comprehensive board-approved compliance and risk frameworks.
  *Source Attribution:* `RBI Scale Based Regulation (SBR) Circular | rbi.org.in`

- **PMLA Beneficial Ownership:** Financial institutions face severe enforcement for incomplete Ultimate Beneficial Ownership (UBO) records.
  *Source Attribution:* `Financial Intelligence Unit - India (FIU-IND) Guidelines | fiuindia.gov.in`

- **SEBI LODR Disclosures:** Listed capital market intermediaries face immediate stock exchange trading curbs for delayed material events.
  *Source Attribution:* `SEBI Listing Obligations & Disclosure Regulations | sebi.gov.in`

- **Master IT Direction 2023:** RBI requires regulated entities to maintain digital operational resilience and 24/7 cybersecurity operations.
  *Source Attribution:* `RBI Master Direction on Information Technology Governance | rbi.org.in`

- **Institutional Audit Readiness:** Continuous regulatory mapping and automated audit trail generation ensure seamless RBI & SEBI supervisory reviews.
  *Source Attribution:* `GOVENICS BFSI & Regulatory Advisory 2024 | govnics-murex.vercel.app`
