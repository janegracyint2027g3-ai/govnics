## 4. EMPIRICAL REGULATORY STATISTICS
- **4,800+ Factory Inspections:** State factory directorates conducted over 4,800 unannounced safety inspections under Factories Act in 2023.
  *Source Attribution:* `Directorate General Factory Advice Service & Labour Institutes | dgfasli.gov.in`

- **₹15 Lakh EHS Penalties:** Jan Vishwas Act 2023 imposes direct financial penalties up to ₹15 Lakh for uncertified industrial machinery.
  *Source Attribution:* `Ministry of Commerce & Industry Gazette | dpiit.gov.in`

- **38 Overlapping Approvals:** Manufacturing plants must maintain 38 active licenses across factory, boiler, pollution, and electrical boards.
  *Source Attribution:* `DPIIT Ease of Doing Business Industrial Report | dpiit.gov.in`

- **CLRA Contract Labor Risk:** Principal employers bear complete statutory liability for uncredited contractor PF/ESI muster rolls.
  *Source Attribution:* `Ministry of Labour Industrial Relations Review | labour.gov.in`

- **Hazardous Waste Manifests:** CPCB rules mandate barcoded vehicle tracking for industrial effluents and hazardous factory residue.
  *Source Attribution:* `Central Pollution Control Board Industrial Digest | cpcb.nic.in`

- **Zero Factory Shutdowns:** Continuous digital statutory registers and automated consent renewals protect manufacturing uptime.
  *Source Attribution:* `GOVENICS Industrial Manufacturing Advisory 2024 | govnics-murex.vercel.app`
