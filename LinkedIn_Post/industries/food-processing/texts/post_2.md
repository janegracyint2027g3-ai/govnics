What should Procurement Leaders demand to see before approving quarterly compliance certifications?

3. Quantified Operational Risk Scoring: Real-time visibility into high-impact regulatory failure modes before external enforcement strikes.

When independent directors and Procurement Leaders have direct line-of-sight into ground-level evidence, audit defensibility shifts from reactive defense to strategic asset preservation.
