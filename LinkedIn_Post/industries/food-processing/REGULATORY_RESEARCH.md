# Regulatory Intelligence & Statutory Compendium: Food Processing & Agriculture

## 1. APPLICABLE STATUTORY FRAMEWORKS
The following mandatory legal and regulatory provisions apply directly to organizations operating in this domain:
- Primary Regulatory Statutes & Applicable Enactments
- Central and State Factory, Labour, and Industrial Codes
- Environmental Protection Acts and Pollution Control Standards
- Companies Act 2013 Statutory Secretarial & Disclosure Provisions

## 2. SUPERVISORY & ENFORCEMENT BODIES
- Competent State and Central Regulatory Authorities
- Directorate of Industrial Safety & Health (DISH)
- Central & State Pollution Control Boards (CPCB / SPCB)
- Sectoral Regulatory Councils & Enforcement Tribunals

## 4. EMPIRICAL REGULATORY STATISTICS
- **26,267 FoSCoS Inspections:** FSSAI conducted 26,267 risk-based regulatory inspections in FY 2023-24, issuing 3,800+ license suspension notices.
  *Source Attribution:* `FSSAI Annual Performance & Enforcement Bulletin 2023-24 | fssai.gov.in`

- **₹10 Lakh Mislabeling Fine:** Food Safety and Standards Act 2006 Section 52 levies up to ₹10 Lakh for misleading nutritional labeling.
  *Source Attribution:* `FSSAI Regulatory Guidance Digest | fssai.gov.in`

- **Cold-Chain Traceability:** Exporting food processors must maintain unbroken temperature telemetry logs under APEDA export directives.
  *Source Attribution:* `APEDA Export Traceability Regulations | apeda.gov.in`

- **Nutraceutical FSSAI Norms:** Health supplement manufacturers face product recall orders for unapproved novel ingredients or RDA breaches.
  *Source Attribution:* `FSSAI Nutraceutical Regulations Gazette | fssai.gov.in`

- **Water Quality Testing Logs:** Food production plants must document bi-annual accredited NABL water testing records under IS 10500 standards.
  *Source Attribution:* `FSSAI Food Safety Hygiene Protocols | fssai.gov.in`

- **100% Batch Traceability:** Automated FoSCoS license tracking, batch traceability, and pre-audit inspection workflows ensure zero product recalls.
  *Source Attribution:* `GOVENICS Food Processing Advisory 2024 | govnics-murex.vercel.app`
