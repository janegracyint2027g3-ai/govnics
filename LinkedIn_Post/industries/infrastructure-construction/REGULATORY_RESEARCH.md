## 4. EMPIRICAL REGULATORY STATISTICS
- **₹18,500 Cr RERA Escrow:** Real estate regulators enforce 70% project revenue escrow under Section 4(2)(l)(D) of RERA Act.
  *Source Attribution:* `Ministry of Housing & Urban Affairs RERA Reports | mohua.gov.in`

- **BOCW Welfare Cess 1%:** Builders face compounding interest for failing to assess and deposit 1% Building and Other Construction Workers cess.
  *Source Attribution:* `BOCW Act Enforcement Bulletin | labour.gov.in`

- **EIA Environmental Clearance:** Infrastructure projects without valid Environmental Impact Assessment (EIA) clearances face NGT demolition stays.
  *Source Attribution:* `National Green Tribunal (NGT) Digest | ngt.gov.in`

- **Workplace Accident Liability:** Section 304A criminal charges apply to site leadership for fatal falls and missing fall-protection netting.
  *Source Attribution:* `DGFASLI Construction Safety Regulations | dgfasli.gov.in`

- **Structural Stability NOC:** Civic corporations mandate periodic structural audits and certified civil engineer endorsements for large sites.
  *Source Attribution:* `National Building Code 2016 Review | bis.gov.in`

- **Flawless Project Compliance:** Integrated RERA escrow tracking, BOCW muster audits, and EIA monitoring prevent project stop-work notices.
  *Source Attribution:* `GOVENICS Infrastructure & Real Estate Advisory 2024 | govnics-murex.vercel.app`
