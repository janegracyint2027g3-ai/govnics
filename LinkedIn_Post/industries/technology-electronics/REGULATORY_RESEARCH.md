## 4. EMPIRICAL REGULATORY STATISTICS
- **₹250 Cr DPDP Penalty:** The DPDP Act 2023 enforces penalties up to ₹250 Crore for failure to implement reasonable security safeguards.
  *Source Attribution:* `DPDP Act 2023 Official Gazette | meity.gov.in`

- **6-Hour CERT-In Rule:** Technology and SaaS providers must report ransomware and infrastructure breaches within 6 hours to CERT-In.
  *Source Attribution:* `CERT-In Cyber Security Directions | cert-in.org.in`

- **E-Waste EPR Mandate:** Electronics manufacturers face severe penalties and import license revocations for missing e-waste collection quotas.
  *Source Attribution:* `CPCB E-Waste Management Rules Gazette | cpcb.nic.in`

- **SEZ STPI Audit Scrutiny:** Tax authorities intensify audits on software export billing and cross-border transfer pricing documentation.
  *Source Attribution:* `CBDT & STPI Regulatory Compliance Compendium | cbdt.gov.in`

- **ISO 27001 Vendor Audit:** Over 75% of Fortune 500 tech contracts require audited SOC 2 Type II reports and Zero Trust cloud posture.
  *Source Attribution:* `Gartner IT Governance & Risk Report 2023 | gartner.com`

- **100% Cyber Defensibility:** Unified DPDP compliance, CERT-In logging, and SOC 2 frameworks ensure frictionless enterprise enterprise sales.
  *Source Attribution:* `GOVENICS Technology & Cyber Practice 2024 | govnics-murex.vercel.app`
