## 4. EMPIRICAL REGULATORY STATISTICS
- **Renewable RPO Mandates:** State electricity regulatory commissions penalize open-access industrial users for missing green power RPO quotas.
  *Source Attribution:* `Ministry of Power RPO Compliance Gazette | powermin.gov.in`

- **₹3 Cr Daily Outage Risk:** Power transmission and distribution grids face steep CERC tariff penalties for uncertified maintenance outages.
  *Source Attribution:* `Central Electricity Regulatory Commission (CERC) | cercind.gov.in`

- **Fly Ash 100% Utilization:** Thermal power stations must maintain 100% fly ash recycling under strict CPCB environmental notifications.
  *Source Attribution:* `CPCB Fly Ash Utilization Compendium | cpcb.nic.in`

- **Mining Safety DGMS Audits:** Directorate General of Mines Safety enforces strict statutory audits on blasting and explosive magazine licenses.
  *Source Attribution:* `DGMS Circulars & Enforcement Register | dgms.gov.in`

- **Carbon Credit Trading:** The Energy Conservation (Amendment) Act creates domestic carbon credit mechanisms requiring audited emission data.
  *Source Attribution:* `Bureau of Energy Efficiency (BEE) Notifications | beeindia.gov.in`

- **100% Utility Compliance:** Automated RPO tracking, environmental telemetry, and safety register audits maintain operational continuity.
  *Source Attribution:* `GOVENICS Energy & Utilities Advisory 2024 | govnics-murex.vercel.app`
