## 4. EMPIRICAL REGULATORY STATISTICS
- **Revised Schedule M Mandate:** CDSCO enforces Revised Schedule M (GMP) standards, mandating automated HVAC qualification and data integrity.
  *Source Attribution:* `CDSCO Gazette Notification G.S.R. 922(E) | cdsco.gov.in`

- **Bio-Medical Waste Barcodes:** Hospitals face immediate cancellation of operating pollution consents for untracked yellow-bag clinical waste.
  *Source Attribution:* `CPCB Bio-Medical Waste Management Rules | cpcb.nic.in`

- **₹5 Lakh DPCO Penalties:** Pharmaceutical manufacturers face statutory overcharging recovery and heavy penalties under DPCO price caps.
  *Source Attribution:* `National Pharmaceutical Pricing Authority (NPPA) | nppa.gov.in`

- **Clinical Establishments Act:** Healthcare facilities must maintain accredited nursing ratios and publish diagnostic tariff schedules under state laws.
  *Source Attribution:* `Ministry of Health & Family Welfare Guidelines | mohfw.gov.in`

- **21 CFR Part 11 Audit Trail:** Diagnostic labs and clinical research organizations must maintain tamper-proof audit trails for all patient testing.
  *Source Attribution:* `Indian Council of Medical Research (ICMR) Standards | icmr.gov.in`

- **Zero Regulatory Sanctions:** End-to-end Schedule M gap audits, medical waste tracking, and clinical governance ensure flawless accreditation.
  *Source Attribution:* `GOVENICS Healthcare & Pharma Practice 2024 | govnics-murex.vercel.app`
