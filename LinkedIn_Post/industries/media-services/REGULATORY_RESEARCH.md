## 4. EMPIRICAL REGULATORY STATISTICS
- **IT Intermediary Rule 3(1):** Digital media platforms and publishers must establish grievance redressal mechanisms with 15-day resolution mandates.
  *Source Attribution:* `Information Technology Rules 2021 | meity.gov.in`

- **UGC AICTE Accreditation:** Higher education institutions face regulatory derecognition for missing statutory faculty-student compliance ratios.
  *Source Attribution:* `University Grants Commission Regulatory Compendium | ugc.ac.in`

- **Hospitality Fire NOC Scrutiny:** Hotels and convention venues face unannounced municipal inspections and license suspensions for fire safety lapses.
  *Source Attribution:* `State Fire Prevention & Life Safety Measures Acts | mha.gov.in`

- **Broadcast Cable TV Act:** Television channels and distribution networks must comply with the Programme and Advertising Code under MIB directives.
  *Source Attribution:* `Ministry of Information & Broadcasting Advisory | mib.gov.in`

- **Aviation Safety DGCA Audits:** Airport concessionaires and commercial airlines undergo rigorous DGCA ramp safety and regulatory audit checks.
  *Source Attribution:* `Directorate General of Civil Aviation Operations Bulletin | dgca.gov.in`

- **Complete Service Defensibility:** Unified statutory licensing, intellectual property safeguards, and grievance compliance protect enterprise reputations.
  *Source Attribution:* `GOVENICS Media & Professional Services Advisory 2024 | govnics-murex.vercel.app`
